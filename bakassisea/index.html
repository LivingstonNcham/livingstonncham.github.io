BakassiSea

A free daily sea-state and safety guide for small-scale fishers on the Bakassi coast, Cameroon. Project by Livingston, University of Dschang.

Mean surface currents, 19 to 25 October 2026

Data: Copernicus Marine Service (hourly merged surface currents, 0.083 degree model grid); GEBCO 2026; basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Where the project stands

  • Phase 1 (complete): three forecast points over water, draft SAFE / CAUTION / AVOID thresholds, a daily forecast script, and six daily forecasts compared.
  • Phase 2 (in progress): currents and sea-floor depth maps.
  • Next: a tidal-current model of the Cross River estuary and a public dashboard.

Advisory only. This is not an official forecast. The thresholds are drafts and still need review with fishers and local fisheries services.
